## Direction for tap2work

Keep the confirmed 06:00 half-hour week grid, role lanes, drag assignment and tap alternatives. On phones, lead with the chosen day, scheduled people, and explicit unassigned required slots. Keep previous/next week and today controls visible. Use small Cupertino-style line icons with text or tooltips, gentle role accents with text labels, and readable touch targets. The prototype saves shared demo shifts; it does not publish a schedule or send staff notifications.
