# Application language fonts

Noto Sans Thai and Noto Sans Devanagari variable fonts from the official Google Fonts repository, retrieved 2026-10-10. Both are bundled under SIL OFL 1.1; licenses are included beside the fonts.

- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansthai
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansdevanagari

Thai and Nepali fallbacks supplement the existing Pretendard, Noto Sans SC and Noto Sans JP fonts. Native rendering/device verification is separate from asset and Flutter layout checks.
